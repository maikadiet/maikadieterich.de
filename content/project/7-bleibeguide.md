The Bleibeguide is a collection of resources meant to facilitate access to the city of Basel and righ of stay, regardless of barriers such as language, income or lega status. The collection has existed for over a decade and has been cared for by many people, amongst others the Bleiberechtkollektiv and the Anlaufstelle für Sans-Papiers. The last update is many years ago, and together with Luca and others, and with the help of Catapult, we are in the process of remaking the website and the collection.
