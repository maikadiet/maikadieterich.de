“To Fold Dough Around Filling” was an event series of interactive gatherings centered on food as a shared practice. While some events focused on the hands-on process of making dumplings like Korean mandu, German Maultaschen, and Polish pierogi, others were spaces for conversation. We spent time sitting together, eating, talking, and writing about food and memory.

Across five events at the Food Cultures Lab, Civic, and Symbiont, we invited students, staff, faculty, and administration to slow down and share a table. Dumplings are a universal tradition, but they take time and effort to make. By sharing both the labor of cooking and the space for reflection, the series became a way to find common ground and connect across differences, building community through the simple act of care that is cooking for one another.
